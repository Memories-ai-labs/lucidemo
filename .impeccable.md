# LUCI Design Context

## Product
AI assistant desktop app (macOS) — Tauri + React + TypeScript

## Target Audience
CEOs and high-volume sales professionals. People in back-to-back meetings all day who need an AI that captures, remembers, and helps them act on their work without slowing them down.

## Core Value Proposition
"Your AI that remembers your work and helps you act on it." Captures meeting context, surfaces action items, connects to the tools you already use.

## Brand Personality
Warm, energetic, clever. Not cold or robotic. Feels like a sharp assistant who knows you well.

## Emotional Goal
Like having a capable, trusted assistant. Users should feel confident and calm — not overwhelmed. The interface should reduce cognitive load, not add to it.

## Aesthetic Direction
Minimal, high-tech, elegant. Light mode primary. Think: sophisticated consumer app, not generic SaaS dashboard. Clean but not sterile.

## Accent Color
#FF9F1C — warm amber/orange. Distinctive. Not blue like every other AI tool.

## Current Stack
- SF Pro Display / Inter (typography)
- White background, #1a1a1a text
- 32px window radius, 22px sidebar radius, 999px button radius
- Floating sidebar with backdrop blur
